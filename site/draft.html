Starter board below blends two independent 2026 sources, both snapshotted Sep 1, 2026: FantasyPros' PPR consensus rankings (100 experts) and ESPN's own projected-points rankings — each player's overall rank is the average of the two where ESPN ranked them, with real bye weeks and FantasyPros' own tiers, and "value"/"sexy pick" flags computed from where the two sources disagree (see draft.html's inline comments if you want the exact rule). Both drift as the preseason goes on, so paste a fresh cheat-sheet CSV here the morning of your draft if it's been a while — format is name,pos,team,tier,flags,bye (flags optional, pipe-separated, e.g. anchor or rookie|trap; bye optional).
